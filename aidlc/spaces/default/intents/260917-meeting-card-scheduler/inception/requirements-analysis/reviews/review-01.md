### Summary

The functional and non-functional requirements are, on the whole, complete, well-organized, and traceable — every FR/NFR either cites an original description clause, a specific confirmed interview answer (Q1–Q8), or an affirmed team practice, and the newly introduced FR4 (Meetings List) is explicitly flagged with its origin per the Inception traceability rule, exactly as required. No requirement reads as fabricated with no source. The two Major findings above are gaps rather than contradictions the artifact tried to paper over — a QR-content edge case left undefined (R-01) and a confirmed 5-minute success-metric answer that never became a testable requirement (R-02) — both with clear, low-effort fixes, plus two Minor testability nits (R-03, R-04) already partly self-flagged by the artifact's own Open Questions section. This keeps the artifact at READY for an advisory pass, with these four points worth the human's attention before Functional Design begins.
