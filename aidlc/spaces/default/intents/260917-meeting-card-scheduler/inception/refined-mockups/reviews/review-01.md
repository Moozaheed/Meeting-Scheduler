### Summary

The interaction spec, design-system mapping, and accessibility checklist are strong work: the component-spec-template format is followed faithfully with concrete, non-boilerplate accessibility fields (specific `aria-live` behavior, real focus-management rules, keyboard alternatives to drag reordering), the accessibility checklist targets WCAG 2.1 AA with specific and checkable items rather than vague ones, and every confirmed Q1–Q6 interview answer is correctly reflected in the mockups. However, the mockups do not cover every functional requirement from `requirements.md` as the stage requires — FR6 (Data Persistence & Clear My Data) is the one FR with no UI representation at all, and its "clear my data" action is an explicit, mandated (`project.md` ALWAYS) requirement, not a nice-to-have. Combined with the missing NFR5.2 PII-disclosure placement, this is a real implementability gap a developer would hit immediately, so this pass is NOT-READY; the two omissions are narrow and should be quick to close in a follow-up revision.
