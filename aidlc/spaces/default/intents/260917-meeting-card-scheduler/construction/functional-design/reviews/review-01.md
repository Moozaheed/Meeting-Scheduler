### Summary

The entity model, business rules, and workflows are largely coherent and well-traced back to `requirements.md`, and the affirmed team practices (layering mandate, lighter card-export test depth, "clear my data" mandate, PII disclosure) are all correctly reflected. However, three Major issues block readiness: two business rules are entirely absent from the traceability file with no explanation (R-01), an explicit requirement that assigned a design decision to this very stage (card dimensions, FR5.2) was punted further downstream instead of resolved (R-02), and the functional spec's workflow narrative contradicts the rules file's own stated validation triggers for two rules (R-03). These are concrete, evidence-grounded gaps a developer would hit while implementing Workflow 1 and the card export path, and they should be fixed before this artifact set gates forward.
